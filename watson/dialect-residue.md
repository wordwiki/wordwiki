# Dialect residue: Rand/Watson vs modern Listuguj

Counterpart pairs ('~rand-mmo-pair') AFTER the systematic transliteration
rules, classified.  Rand recorded Nova Scotia-area speech ~140 years ago;
the dictionary records Listuguj today.  Once spelling-system differences
are removed by rules, what remains mixes: length-mark practice (A - not
dialect), REAL sound differences (C vowel quality, D uvularity, E
epenthesis - the dialect/diachrony signal), different inflections picked
as the headword (F - not dialect), and unsorted (G).  The hub prediction
(via watson-sf) is used where the entry carries it, the li spoke
otherwise.  FOR REVIEW: C and D are the tables worth expert eyes.

Caveat: all C/D/E rows are MEDIUM-confidence pairings (near-skeleton +
definition overlap) - by construction, since high-confidence pairs have
identical skeletons and can only differ in marks.  A medium pairing is
occasionally a wrong match rather than dialect; the definition gate
limits this, but reviewers should read the pair, not just the letters.
