My dictionary project consists of four dictionaries.

1 The mmolistuguj2024 consists of 6,816 entries.
It has the following fields: \lx, the lexeme spelling in the Ng system \lsf, the entry in the
Lk system spelling \ws occurs four times where some entries have a Mi’gmaw
pronounces the head word, \ph (pronunciation help with the word divided into syllables),
\ps the part of speech in Mi’gmewi’suti system, \pn part of speech in the English system,
\ge the gloss gives an English translation of the head word, \de a literal translation of the
head word in English, \xv a sentence in Mi’gmewi’suti, \xe the translation of that
sentence, \lf a code for an example, \lv that example in the vernacular, \le the translation
of the vernacular word, \sd semantic domain and \dt the date the entry was last
adjusted.

2 RandMigEngDict33276
This is the Rand dictionary with the original number of entries as 33,276.
This one has the following fields, \lx the tentative spelling of Rand’s Mi’gmewei word, (all
33,276 words) this involved a tremendous amount of work, but it is done.
The other fields consist of: \lsf the Nk spelling, \ph a pronunciation help, \ps the part of
speech in Mi’gmewi’suti, \pn the part of speech in the English system, \ge the gloss in
English, \xv an example of the word in the vernacular, \xe the translation of the
example, \so contains Rand 1888 p and the page number where the entry is found in
Rand’s dictionary, \nt any notes and \dt the date it was last adjusted.
This dictionary is being converted to the Ng dictionary for the Source dialect people, and
secondly the Lk dictionary for the Goal dialect people. Currently this dictionary on July
20, 2026, has 29,096 entries.

3 RandMigmFinal2000.
This is the Ng version. Currently there are 2,498 entries.
The fields are: \lx, \lsf, \ph, \ps, \pn. \ge, \xv, \so. \nt.
Here is an example:
\lx agnutas’g
\lsf aknutasɨk
\ph a g` nu da s`k

\pn vii
\ge it is told

To move a record from RandMi’gmEngDict33278.
1 Select a word in that database. I selected enaqatg.
2 Choose Database&gt;Move.
3 Select RandMi’gmFinal2000.
4 Press OK. This moves the entry to RandMi’gmFinal2000.
5 Then enter the appropriate data in the fields of that entry.
6 Select Database&gt;Copy.
7 Select RandMi’kmFinal2000.
8 Press OK. This puts the entry in RandMi’kmFinal2000.

9 Put the cursor before the e in enaqatk in \lsf.
10 Hit backspace until the word enaqatk is in \lx.
Following these steps makes two copies of the entry. One for the Ng database and the
other for the Lk database.
This is how I make two new dictionaries. One for the Ng area and one for the Lk area.
