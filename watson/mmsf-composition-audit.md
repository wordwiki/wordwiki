# The mm-li → mm-sf composition audit

Two independent routes to mm-sf, compared (transliteration-findings.md
Part 3): the DIRECT rules-v4 engine (learned from ~1.5k team pairs) and
the HUB-JUSTIFIED mapping (only what Rand's phonetically complete
orthography licenses: g→k voicing + final/prevocalic -ei→-ey; the schwa
apostrophe round-trips, and Watson's archaisms are conventions, not
phonetics).  Where the routes agree AGAINST a human spelling, that
spelling is the suspect; where v4 alone is right, we are looking at a
TEAM CONVENTION beyond the phonetics; where the hub alone is right, a
v4 rule overreached.
